✈️ About ConnectChat

A modern messaging app to connect with your friends, classmates and community.

🛡️ Your Privacy, Our Priority

Messages are stored securely in MongoDB and protected in transit with HTTPS. They are not end-to-end encrypted. You control who sees your online status, and you can block, report or delete your account any time.
