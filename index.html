🏛️ About JMI

Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) was founded on 29 October 1920 at Aligarh by nationalist leaders including Maulana Mohammad Ali Jauhar, Hakim Ajmal Khan, Mukhtar Ahmed Ansari, Abdul Majeed Khwaja and Dr. Zakir Husain. In Urdu, Jamia means “university” and Millia means “national”.

  • Moved from Aligarh to Delhi in 1925; the present Okhla campus was started in the 1930s.
  • Became a Central University by an Act of Parliament on 26 December 1988.
  • Declared a minority institution and accredited by NAAC (A++ as per the 2025-26 Students’ Handbook).
  • Located at Jamia Nagar, Okhla, New Delhi – 110025.
  • Offers UG, PG, diploma, certificate, doctoral and school programmes across many faculties.

For official news and admissions visit jmi.ac.in.
