🏛️ About JMI

Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) is a public central university in New Delhi, India. The name means “the National Islamic University”, and over the years it has grown into a diverse campus where students from every part of the country study together.

📜 Our history

  • Founded in 1920 at Aligarh during the Non-Cooperation Movement, by leaders including Maulana Mohammad Ali Jauhar, Hakim Ajmal Khan, Dr. Zakir Husain and Mahatma Gandhi’s supporters, as an institution free from colonial control.
  • Moved to Delhi in 1925 and later settled at its present Okhla campus.
  • Became a deemed university in 1962 and a central university in 1988.

📚 What students study

JMI offers a wide range of programmes — engineering, architecture, law, humanities, sciences, education, fine arts, mass communication and more — from undergraduate to doctoral level.

💚 Why this charity

Many talented JMI students struggle with fees, books and daily expenses. JMI Student Charity is a small student-run effort to bring people together and put voluntary contributions towards education and community needs. Every rupee, big or small, helps a student stay on track.
