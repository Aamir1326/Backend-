Why French is good at JMI

🎓 A proper degree programme

JMI’s Department of Foreign Languages offers BA (Hons.) French and Francophone Studies, so you can study French as a full major and not only as a side course.

📚 What you study

  • Communicative French: oral comprehension and expression, reading and writing skills
  • Introduction to French and Francophone literature
  • French-speaking world and cultural history of the French
  • French through media
  • French for specific purposes, such as commercial French
  • Optional consecutive interpretation courses

🌍 Real-world exposure

JMI has hosted workshops with the Embassy of France in New Delhi (for example, “Learning French in Jamia Millia Islamia: Future Prospects”), which connect students with French cooperation and career opportunities.

💼 Careers

French opens doors in translation and interpretation, teaching, tourism and hospitality, international companies, publishing, media and diplomacy-related work.

✅ Other advantages

  • Central university with low public-university fees
  • Part-time certificate and diploma courses in French are also offered
  • Delhi location with access to French institutions and cultural events

Eligibility, seats and dates change every year. Please confirm on jmi.ac.in.
